Key Legal & Seating Insights

  • 1.
    Karnataka bans 17-seater Tempo Travellers: Only 17-seater Force Urbania is allowed because it is factory-fitted, whereas 17-seater Tempo Travellers are modified after-market. Reference
  • 2.
    Child Lap Rule (<3 Years Only): Legally, only children under 3 years old may sit on adults' laps. Our 7 kids are aged 5 to 9 years, so all 16 travelers require individual seats (eliminating 13-seater and 14-seater options). Reference

Key Trip Assumptions Factored In

  • Full-Time AC (Except Ghat Section): Even though December weather in Wayanad is pleasant, AC will stay ON throughout the journey (except steep ghat climbs) to keep windows closed against unexpected rain.
  • Overhead Luggage Carrier + Rain Cover: Essential for 5 families' 4-day luggage so cabin legroom stays comfortable and bags stay dry.
  • No 21-Seater Mini Buses: Ruled out based on previous group experience (wobbly ride and slow speed on hill roads).
